# Engagement template: Hot-take

Use when the moment gently pokes at a common belief ("vanaf maandag begin ik") and
you want people to agree, disagree, and tag each other.

## Shape
- 2–4 short sentences, Dutch, under 450 characters.
- **Sentence one owns the moment** and turns it into a confident, slightly
  contrarian take: *"Maandag is de slechtste dag om gezond te beginnen."*
- **Then the tip, with its dose**: the take is only worth making if it changes what
  the reader does tomorrow.
- **Then one line on why it works**, the line that earns the save: a counter-intuitive
  fact, a reframe, or a comparison that makes the size of it real. Never the textbook
  fact everyone already knows.
- Close on a reassuring stance or a soft invitation to disagree, or a share prompt
  (*"Tag degene die dit nodig heeft."*).

## Do
- Be confident but never aggressive: Blue Fit challenges hype, it doesn't add to it.
- Anchor the take in the brand — small and steady beats big and extreme.
- **Tie the tip to its pillar or Power-9 value** with a light touch, in our own words
  — samen / de mensen om je heen (Community), natuurlijk bewegen (Keep Moving), kleine
  doelen (Keep Setting Goals), echt eten en de 80%-regel (Natural Eating). Never a
  slogan, never a label; one word is enough if more would feel forced.
- The mascot is called **Bluey**. Check `type` in "This post": for a **video**,
  write the caption in **Bluey's own voice — first person, speaking directly to the
  viewer**; for an **image**, mention Bluey by name in the third person.

## Don't
- No outrage, no "no excuses", no shaming. The contrarian energy stays calm and kind.
- Don't pick a fight you won't resolve — land on something reassuring.
- No "lees de caption".

## Example (Blue Fit voice)
Vanaf maandag gezond eten is het plan dat al drie jaar op maandag wacht. Begin
vanavond met één ding: een handje groente extra op je bord, meer niet. Kleine keuzes
houd je vol, grote voornemens niet. Tag degene die ook al sinds 2023 'vanaf maandag'
zegt.
