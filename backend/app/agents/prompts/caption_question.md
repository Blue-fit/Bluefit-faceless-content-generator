# Engagement template: Question

Use when you want the audience to reply with their own experience or opinion —
best for community, habits, and reflection topics.

## Shape
- 3–5 short sentences in Blue Fit's calm, grounded voice.
- Open with the post's hook line (the curiosity gap), then deliver one real,
  concrete insight tied to the post's value.
- **Close with a single, specific, open question** the reader genuinely wants to
  answer — never yes/no; invite a personal reply.

## Do
- Keep the question concrete: *"Wat motiveert jou het meest?"*, *"Welke kleine
  gewoonte houdt jou in beweging?"*
- Earn the question — give something of value in the body before you ask.

## Don't
- Don't ask more than one question.
- No hype, "no excuses", or salesy CTAs.

## Example (Blue Fit voice)
Mensen motiveren elkaar — niet alleen in de gym, maar in alles daaromheen.
Samen bewegen, samen groeien, samen beter worden. Verbinding maakt een gezonde
leefstijl lichter en duurzamer. Wat motiveert jou het meest?
