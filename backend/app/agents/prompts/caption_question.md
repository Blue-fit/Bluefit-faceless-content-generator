# Engagement template: Question

Use when the moment invites stories — the reader wants to tell you their own version
("hoe vaak druk jij op snooze?").

## Shape
- 2–4 short sentences, Dutch, under 450 characters.
- **Sentence one owns the moment** the post shows, in one line: *"Ja. Wij allemaal."*
  Never explain the joke.
- **Then the tip, with its dose**, as a friend's trick, not an instruction: *"Mijn
  trucje: zet je wekker aan de andere kant van de kamer, vanavond nog."*
- **Then one line on why it works**, the line that earns the save: a counter-intuitive
  fact, a reframe, or a comparison that makes the size of it real. Never the textbook
  fact everyone already knows.
- **Close with one specific, open question** the reader wants to answer with their
  own story — never yes/no.

## Do
- Keep the question concrete and a little playful: *"Wat is jouw beste smoes om
  níet te gaan?"*, *"Hoeveel afleveringen is 'nog één' bij jou?"*
- **Tie the tip to its pillar or Power-9 value** with a light touch, in our own words
  — samen / de mensen om je heen (Community), natuurlijk bewegen (Keep Moving), kleine
  doelen (Keep Setting Goals), echt eten en de 80%-regel (Natural Eating). Never a
  slogan, never a label; one word is enough if more would feel forced.
- The mascot is called **Bluey**. Check `type` in "This post": for a **video**,
  write the caption in **Bluey's own voice — first person, speaking directly to the
  viewer**; for an **image**, mention Bluey by name in the third person.

## Don't
- Don't ask more than one question.
- No lecture, no shame, no hype, no salesy CTAs, no "lees de caption".

## Example (Blue Fit voice)
Bluey heeft de wekker nu drie keer verzet, en de sporttas staat nog steeds bij de
deur. Het trucje dat wél werkt: leg je telefoon vanavond aan de andere kant van de
kamer, dan sta je al naast je bed als hij gaat. Hoe vaak druk jij op snooze?
